## Trading (stocks, crypto and futures)

- **You can lose money.** The value of investments can fall as well as rise, and you may get back less than you put in.
- **Past performance:** it does not predict future results.
- **Futures** use leverage: a small price move can cause large losses, which can be more than the money you put in.
- **Crypto-assets:**
  - They are highly volatile and largely unregulated in some countries.
  - They can fall to zero.
  - They aren't protected by deposit insurance or investor compensation schemes.
- **US stocks:** they carry currency risk, US taxes (such as withholding on dividends) and market-hours differences.
- **Demo trading** uses virtual money. Real markets behave differently, including price gaps, slippage and your own emotions.
- **Real trading** is provided by [BROKER] under its own terms. Your investments are held with that broker, not Aurex.
