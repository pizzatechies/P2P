## How to complain

Contact us any way you like:

- in the app: [Help → Contact support];
- by email: [COMPLAINTS EMAIL];
- by phone: [PHONE], [HOURS];
- by post: [ADDRESS].

Tell us your name, your @handle or account number, what happened, and what you'd like us to do. Never send us your password or two-factor codes.

## What happens next

1. **We acknowledge your complaint** within [1] business day and give you a reference number.
2. **We investigate.** A person who wasn't involved in the original issue looks at it.
3. **We reply in writing** within [15] business days. If it's complex we'll tell you why we need more time, and we will reply within [30] business days at most.
4. **If we got it wrong**, we'll put it right. That may mean refunding money, correcting records, or compensating you where appropriate.

For an unauthorised payment or a suspected account takeover, contact us **immediately**. We'll secure the account first and investigate after.

## If you're not satisfied

If you're unhappy with our final answer, or we haven't replied in time, you can take your complaint to:

- [Kenya: Central Bank of Kenya, Bank Supervision / Consumer Protection, via [CHANNEL]];
- [Zambia: Bank of Zambia, Financial Sector Ombudsman / consumer complaints desk];
- [Nigeria: CBN Consumer Protection Department];
- [Ghana: Bank of Ghana, Consumer Protection and Market Conduct Office];
- [For data-protection complaints: the data protection authority named in our Privacy Policy].

You can also go to court.

## How we learn from complaints

- **Records:** we log every complaint, its cause, the outcome and how long it took. We keep the records for [5] years.
- **Review:** management reviews complaint trends every [month].
- **Reporting:** we report complaints statistics to regulators as required.
